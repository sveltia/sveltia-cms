# Sveltia CMS

[Sveltia CMS](https://sveltiacms.app/) is the leading Git-based headless CMS for static sites. It’s open source, free, and a complete modern rewrite of Netlify CMS, now known as Decap CMS, which has been [neglected for years](https://sveltiacms.app/docs/successor-to-netlify-cms#what-about-decap-cms).

Designed for content editors and developers alike, Sveltia CMS delivers a great UX/DX, [powerful features](https://sveltiacms.app/features), and [first-class internationalization (i18n) support](https://sveltiacms.app/features/i18n) — all in a small, maintenance-free, single-page web application served from a CDN. Its generic-purpose approach makes it suitable for a wide range of projects, from personal blogs and portfolios to marketing sites and knowledge bases.

As the [successor to Netlify/Decap CMS](https://sveltiacms.app/docs/successor-to-netlify-cms), we continue to deal with issues reported in their repository, of which 405 have now been solved in Sveltia CMS (880 including duplicates). It incorporates numerous enhancements while maintaining high compatibility with existing installations. A growing number of projects, including U.S. government agencies, are [switching from Netlify/Decap CMS](https://sveltiacms.app/docs/migration/netlify-decap-cms) to Sveltia CMS to enjoy its significantly improved performance, security, reliability, and experience.

Sveltia CMS is [framework-agnostic](https://sveltiacms.app/docs/frameworks). It’s an excellent option for those moving away from a traditional CMS and looking for a lightweight headless CMS that works well with Astro, Eleventy, or any other static site generator (SSG). It also works well with vanilla JavaScript and custom tooling.

Explore the largest [showcase](https://sveltiacms.app/showcase) of any Git-based CMS with 835 real-world examples, including 215 sites migrated from Netlify/Decap CMS and 125 from WordPress. Try out the CMS on a [demo site](https://demo.sveltiacms.app/) or visit the [documentation](https://sveltiacms.app/docs) to get started.
